    How to read this

    Buyside is the USD value of Uniswap liquidity sitting below the current price, which is what a seller would trade into. Sellside is the liquidity above it, which is what a buyer would trade into. Imbalance is buyside minus sellside.

    This is concentrated AMM liquidity, not a limit-order book, and it doesn't predict where the price will go. Pools quoted in tokens without a USD price feed are listed but left out of the chart. V4 pools with hooks are treated as standard pools.

    Not financial advice. On-chain data and external price feeds can be wrong or stale.
